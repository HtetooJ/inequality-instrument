# Sound credits

The SND01 Sine WAV files in `snd01/` were copied from the user-provided `SND01_sine` folder in `Ko Thin Cha V1`. SND publishes its UI sounds for personal and commercial use under its terms, asks users to link or credit the site when possible, and prohibits redistribution of unprocessed files as a standalone asset. This prototype uses them only as interaction feedback. [SND terms](https://snd.dev/)

SND03 Industrial JP is not redistributed here. When selected, the prototype uses the official SND library's public audio sprite: https://github.com/snd-lib/snd-lib/tree/master/assets/sounds/sprite/03

The Clear bell chime completion cue uses the user-provided `universfield-clear-bell-chime-487898.mp3` asset. Playback uses the first 0.52 seconds with a short fade to retain the initial strike and avoid the long ringing tail.
